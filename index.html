A GENTLE NOTE

Your skin is allowed to change.

Weather, stress, hormones, and new products can all affect how your skin feels. Treat your profile as a starting point, not a fixed label.

Keep the basics close

  • Be gentle when cleansing.
  • Moisturize in a way that feels comfortable.
  • Use broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day.
  • Pause a product if it causes persistent irritation.

For persistent or concerning symptoms, check in with a licensed dermatologist.
